Privacy Policy

SlideCraft: Carousel & Grid · Developer: Thinh Nguyen · Effective date: 2026-10-19

Summary

SlideCraft does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal data. All photo processing happens on your iPhone. The app has no accounts and works fully offline.

Your photos

You choose a photo with the system photo picker, which gives SlideCraft access only to the photo you select — the app cannot see the rest of your library. The photo is processed in memory on your device to create slides. Nothing is sent to any server.

Saving to Photos

When you tap Save, iOS asks for permission to add photos to your library. SlideCraft uses this only to save the slides you created. It does not request permission to read your library.

Data stored on your device

The app stores a few preferences (for example appearance and whether you have seen the introduction) locally using the system's standard UserDefaults storage. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app removes it.

Tracking and third parties

SlideCraft contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
