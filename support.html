How it works

Choose a photo (or try the sample), pick 2–10 slides and an aspect ratio, drag Position to frame it, then tap Preview Swipe to check the seams. Save All to Photos saves files named SlideCraft_01_of_03.jpg and so on, in order. For a 3×3 profile grid, post tile 09 first and 01 last.

Photos won't save?

Open the iOS Settings app › SlideCraft › Photos and allow “Add Photos Only”, then tap Save again.

Language

SlideCraft follows your iPhone language. To change it for SlideCraft only: Settings › SlideCraft › Language.
